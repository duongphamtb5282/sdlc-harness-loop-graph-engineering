# Fraud & Risk Systems

*Detection pipelines, risk scoring, and compliance controls for high-stakes transactions.*

## What it covers

- Fraud detection architectures: rules, scoring models, anomaly detection
- Real-time vs batch risk pipelines: latency budgets, thresholds, review queues
- Data engineering for risk: event streams, feature stores, model monitoring
- Compliance hooks: AML/KYC checkpoints, audit requirements, data retention
- Cost-aware design: scoring at transaction volume without exploding infra bills

## What you receive

- Risk architecture with decision records (rules vs models vs hybrid)
- Pipeline and data-flow designs with failure semantics
- Non-functional requirements: latency, availability, recovery objectives
- Implementation plan with test cases for adversarial scenarios

## When to use it

You process payments, claims, or account activity where fraud costs real money and every false positive costs customers.
