# Security Hardening

*Defense in depth for applications, infrastructure, and the delivery process itself.*

## What it covers

- Application security: input validation, authN/authZ, injection defenses, secrets handling
- Infrastructure security: network segmentation, least privilege, encryption at rest and in transit
- Delivery security: dependency scanning, SBOM, signing, artifact provenance
- Threat modeling: attack surface review, STRIDE-style analysis, risk ranking
- Incident readiness: detection, response runbooks, recovery drills

## What you receive

- Security review with findings ranked by exploitability and impact
- Threat model covering your system's actual attack surface
- Hardening plan: quick wins first, structural changes with ownership
- Verification checklist to prove the controls after remediation

## When to use it

Before a launch, after an audit, or when a security review turned up more than you expected.
